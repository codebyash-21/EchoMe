## Our USP

The only companion that steps in **before** you fail: it sizes each promise to your body and nudges you in your own voice, with all data kept on your device.

## New approach and combination

- Body signals + spoken intent + promise history, read together by a local LLM.
- Behavioural science built in: self-persuasion (your own voice) and if-then plans (implementation intentions).
- Said-vs-data mirror: compares what you say about yourself with what your data shows.
